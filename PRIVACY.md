# Privacy Policy for Civitai Theater

**Last updated**: October 31, 2026

## Overview
Civitai Theater ("the extension") is an open-source browser extension designed to provide a cinema-grade fullscreen media slideshow for digital artworks and videos on Civitai and Decomfy.

## What Data We Collect
**Civitai Theater does not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or user identity information.**

- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, credentials, or demographic data.
- **No Browsing History**: We do not track or store visited websites, browsing habits, or URLs outside of detecting the active media elements needed for the slideshow playback on supported domains.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry**: The extension contains zero third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, etc.) and zero external tracking pixels or scripts.

## How Data Is Stored
- **Local Storage Only**: The extension uses Chrome's local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`) strictly to remember your user preferences locally on your own machine (such as slideshow speed, volume mute state, and shuffle setting). This data never leaves your device.

## Network & Permissions
- **Host Permissions**: Civitai Theater only operates on Civitai and Decomfy domains to display the media slideshow player and query public generation parameters directly from the platform's public API. No data is transmitted to any third-party servers.
- **Remote Code**: Civitai Theater does not use or load any remote code. 100% of the code is packaged directly within the extension package.
